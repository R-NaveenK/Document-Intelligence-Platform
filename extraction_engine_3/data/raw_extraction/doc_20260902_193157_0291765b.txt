STEPS TO USE THE graVITas’26 Registration Portal
IN CASE OF EXTERNAL PARTICIPANTS
Step 1: Open the gravitas website: https://gravitas.vit.ac.in/
Step 2: Click on the Sign-In tab and choose External Student.
Step 3: This will redirect you to the external registration portal. Click on New User? Register to register as a participant.
Kindly remember the email id and password you use here as all the information will be conveyed to the email address you have used here and this email-id will
be required to sign in later.

Step 4: Enter all the details as required and click on Register Now
Step 5: Click on Register Now
Step 6: Once you get the success pop-up, you will get the screen below. You may continue to login the website with the same email-id and password.
Step 7: You will get a screen below once you have logged in. It is MANDATORY to upload Institution ID and Recent Photograph. Only if all documents are
uploaded, you will be able to register for an event. NOC can be updated at any time but the physical copy must be bought to VIT Vellore for verification
purposes. Only if all documents are uploaded, you will be able to register for events.

Step 8: Once you have uploaded all the necessary documents, kindly click on My Account and then go to User Profile .
In this screen, you will be able to see your uploaded photograph, Registration ID (GR202600XXXX), Name, Registered Email, Mobile Number and College
Name.
The Registration ID is unique for every single external registered on the portal
Step 9: Go to Registration  Event Registration tab.
Step 10: Choose the event category and event name you wish to participate in.
Step 11: You will be able to choose the number of participants under each event.
 In case of individual participants, you may choose 1 participant and click on register.
-
If it is a paid event, your invoice number is generated, and you may proceed for the payment for that particular event.
-
If it is a free event, click on register and confirm.

 In case of team events (2 or more participants), if you wish to participate an event with your friends, choose the number of participants as per your team
size. You are now the team leader.
-
Enter a team name
-
Enter the External Registration ID Number of all your teammates and verify the names carefully.
-
Once you have filled all the details you may click on Register. Now you are all registered as 1 team.
-
Once this is done, your invoice number is generated, and you may proceed with the payment for that particular event.
-
Please note that the team leader only has to pay for all the team members, combined.
-
If it is a paid event, your invoice number is generated, and you may proceed for the payment for that event.
-
If it is a free event, click on register and confirm.
Step 12: Once the payment is completed, you are successfully registered as a team for the event you have chosen. You will get a receipt which includes the
transaction details. Kindly download and save this receipt for future safety in case of discrepancies.
POINTS TO BE NOTED
1. While entering VIT Vellore, please make sure to carry the following:
o Physical Institution ID
o Physical NOC
o Copy of the transaction receipt
2. We will not be requiring any government issued ID card, but we request you to always keep one in hand.
3. For any additional assistance, you may also contact the General Enquiry Team at enquiry.gravitas@vit.ac.in
